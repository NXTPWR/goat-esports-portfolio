I'm G.O.A.T — an esports professional active across the Fortnite esports ecosystem. My experience covers executive and management roles, internal affairs, founding communities and supporting organisations at community level.

Working across multiple organisations has given me a practical understanding of how teams are structured, how coordination works and how professional communication holds an organisation together. I have seen what makes a community feel supported — and what causes structure to break down.

Alongside esports, I create content and build websites. That gives me a mix of people skills, creative skills and technical skills that I bring to every organisation I work with. I am also a web developer who enjoys turning ideas into clean, functional digital experiences.
